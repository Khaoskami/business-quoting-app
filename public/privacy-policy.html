Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Summary: Business Quotes App stores your account and business data on our servers so you can access it from any device. We do not sell or share your data, and you can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Who We Are

Business Quotes App is developed by Khaoskami. Contact: github.com/Khaoskami

What We Collect

To provide the service, we collect and store:

Account information — your name, email address, and password. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash; we never see your plaintext password.

Business and quoting data — the business profile, clients, catalog items, and quotes you create in the app.

Subscription information — your current plan (free / pro / business), subscription status, and a PayFast billing reference linking your account to its billing record.

Session information — a session token stored in an httpOnly cookie on your browser so you stay signed in for up to 30 days.

We do not collect tracking analytics, crash reports, location data, or device fingerprints.

Where Your Data Is Stored

All account and application data is stored server-side in a managed PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway. The database and the application server run in the same private network and are not directly accessible from the public internet.

Data is transmitted between your browser and our server over HTTPS (TLS). Authentication is handled by Better Auth using signed, httpOnly, secure session cookies — these cookies cannot be read by JavaScript and are sent only to our domain.

Payments

Payments are processed entirely by PayFast (operated by Network International). When you upgrade, you are redirected to PayFast's hosted checkout. Card numbers and other payment details are entered on PayFast's pages and never reach our servers. We receive only a billing token and subscription status (active, past due, canceled), used to determine which plan you have access to. PayFast's privacy policy applies to your payment interactions with them.

Third Parties

The third-party services that handle your data on our behalf are limited to:

Railway — application and database hosting.

PayFast (Network International) — payment processing (only if you subscribe to a paid plan).

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties.

Data Security

Connections between your browser and the server are encrypted with TLS. Passwords are hashed before storage. Session cookies are httpOnly and Secure. The server enforces that you can only read or modify records belonging to your own account.

Children's Privacy

This app is designed for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect or store any data from children under the age of 13.

Your Rights

You can view and edit your business profile, quotes, clients, and catalog at any time from within the app.

You can delete your account and all associated data (quotes, clients, catalog items, business profile, subscription record) at any time. Deleting your account permanently removes this data from our database. If you have an active paid subscription, cancel it from Settings → Cancel subscription before deleting your account.

To request a copy of your data, or to ask questions about how we handle it, contact us at the address below.
